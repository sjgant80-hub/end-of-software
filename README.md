# The End of Software

**LIVE: https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/end-of-software/**

Software was always a workaround: intent, frozen by someone else, rented back as a cage.
This repo runs the alternative — **the seam**: hold a possibility-space (the shadow),
collapse *capability* out of it **on intent**, use it, fold it back. You don't have
software. You're the node that renders it.

## The seam law — [`render.mjs`](render.mjs) · witness **38/40 + 2 argued, CLEAN**

- **INTENT** — a natural ask becomes checkable tokens, and **five door classes** are caught
  before anything renders: money · legal · irreversible · external · **private** (sididy's
  door, from the design brainstorm: *a leak cannot be folded back*). What lands on a door is
  never auto-collapsed — the human key turns first.
- **COLLAPSE** — resolution in κ arithmetic, in decision order: **REUSE** when one
  crystallized fold covers the intent at κ = 0.618 (*check-the-estate-first as law, not
  discipline — zero new software*); **RECOMBINE** when two folds ≥ κ/2 each together cover
  ≥ κ (*a composition, not a new cage*); **GENERATE** only as the last resort, with a brief
  that names exactly the gap and the nearest folds to imitate. Every κ boundary is pinned
  **to the bit** (309-of-500 and 309+309-of-1000 constructions).
- **PERSIST** — fold-back is the **default**. Freezing a render needs an argument a reviewer
  could dispute: *"persistence is a choice, and a shrug does not freeze a fold."*
- **FOLD-BACK + RECALL** — the render dissolves; a learning record remains, keyed by the
  intent's tokens (rephrasings share a memory). The same intent asked again is answered from
  the ledger first: *the shadow remembers.*

## Three persistences, one mechanism

The "three stages of AI software" are not a roadmap in time — they are the same seam at
three persistence levels, all running here: **keep-frozen** (folds that earned their
persistence), **hold-live** (compositions that exist while used), **render-and-fold**
(capability as a verb — it happens, then un-happens, leaving learning). One mechanism, a
persistence policy per capability.

## The honest wire

The page's generated micro-tools are deterministic renders of the brief — real enough to use
and fold back. This page does **not** claim to write arbitrary software; for that, the brief
hands off to *your* generator (a local model, a resident mind, any sovereign seam). **The law
is the product**: reuse-first, five doors, argued persistence, remembered renders.
