The End of Software

Software was always a workaround: intent, frozen by someone else years ago, rented back to you as a cage. The alternative is running on this page: a seam that holds a possibility-space — 421 live, working folds of one estate — and collapses capability out of it on intent. You say what you need. The seam reuses what exists, recombines what almost exists, generates only as a last resort — and what it generates folds back when you're done, leaving only learning behind. You don't have software. You're the node that renders it.

Three persistences, one mechanism

The three "stages of AI software" are not a roadmap — they are the same seam at three persistence levels, all running here: keep-frozen (the 421 folds — capability that earned its persistence), hold-live (compositions that exist while used), render-and-fold (capability as a verb: it happens, then it un-happens). Software stops being a noun you own and rent, and becomes a verb your node performs.
